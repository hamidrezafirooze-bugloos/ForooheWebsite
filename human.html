The idea

A person, lifted.

At the heart of the mark is a figure with arms open to the sky, hope, recovery, relief. Beneath, a wave carries them: the support that never lets go. The letters and the person become one, because care and the people it serves are inseparable.

What it stands for

Support · Care · Thrive.

Support

Someone in your corner from day one, a supporter only a tap away.

Care

Gentle reminders and guidance that meet patients where they are, never nagging.

Thrive

Progress you can see and feel, recovery that moves forward, together.

Color

One confident blue.

Where the first identity glows with gradients, this one keeps it clean and human, a single trustworthy blue on deep navy.
