The idea

Two shapes that lean into each other.

Zino's mark is two blades that meet in the middle — a patient and a supporter. Read together they form a Z; read closely they become a figure with open arms. It's a logo about being lifted, not just treated.

Built to interlock

One shape, mirrored and turned.

Each blade hooks into its twin, with a soft offset echo behind it for depth — support that's always moving toward you. Scroll to watch them lock.
